HIPAA: Knowledge Base

The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) of 1996 is a set of regulatory standards that intend to protect private and sensitive patient data from hospitals, insurance companies, and healthcare providers.

GDPR: Knowledge Base

The General Data Protection Regulation is a European Union regulation on Information privacy in the European Union and the European Economic Area. The GDPR is an important component of EU privacy law and human rights law, in particular Article 8 of the Charter of Fundamental Rights of the European Union.

SOC: Knowledge Base

SOC is a voluntary compliance standard for service organizations, developed by the American Institute of CPAs (AICPA), which specifies how organizations should manage customer data. The standard is based on the following Trust Services Criteria: security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, privacy.
